# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-07-17

### Added

- Kleine Koe Sovereign OSS Assurance product positioning.
- Expiring repository/check scope manifests and local-only endpoint policy.
- Repository-policy checks and normalized SARIF ingestion.
- Fixed Semgrep, Gitleaks and offline OSV scanner orchestration.
- Bubblewrap network isolation and pinned scanner installers.
- Bounded SARIF/model ingestion and prioritized evidence selection.
- Semantic assurance validation with one bounded repair attempt for prohibited
  compliance, certification and completeness claims.
- Durable SQLite workflow records and retry of failed model attempts.
- Named approval, idempotent local export and workflow status commands.
- Pilot metrics, management summaries and branded evidence packs.
- Loopback-only review dashboard with CSRF and security headers.
- Commercial discovery/pilot templates, operations runbook and data schedule.
- Product landing-page draft and expanded threat model.

### Verified

- Full local scanner suite on the development repository.
- Deterministic end-to-end pilot lifecycle through approved export.
- Local Qwen3.6 35B-A3B assessment with exact citations and bounded language.

### Limitations

- Single-node pilot architecture, not multi-tenant production hosting.
- Synthetic evaluation set; no customer accuracy or time-saved claim.
- Local approved export only; no external ticket/Forgejo connector enabled.
