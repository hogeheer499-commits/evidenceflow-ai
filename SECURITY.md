# Security policy

EvidenceFlow is a security-sensitive prototype maintained by Kleine Koe. Do not
include real secrets, unpublished customer findings or personal data in a public
issue.

Report a suspected vulnerability privately through the contact route at
https://kleinekoe.nl/contact and mark the message **Confidential security
report — EvidenceFlow**. Include the affected version, impact, reproduction
conditions and the minimum evidence needed to validate the issue.

Please do not perform active testing against Kleine Koe or customer production
systems without explicit written authorization. Testing a local copy of this
repository with synthetic data is welcome.

We aim to acknowledge a complete report within five business days. Timing for
validation and remediation depends on severity, reproducibility and affected
deployments. This policy does not promise a bounty or authorize activity outside
the law or an agreed scope.
