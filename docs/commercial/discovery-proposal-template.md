## Executive decision

Kleine Koe proposes a time-boxed discovery to determine whether a local,
evidence-first assurance workflow can reduce scanner triage effort and improve
remediation evidence without sending source code or unpublished findings to a
public AI provider.

## Objectives

- identify the problem owner, budget owner and operational owner;
- authorize a candidate set of 10–20 repositories and permitted checks;
- map scanner feeds, ownership, ticketing, retention and disclosure routes;
- establish the threat model and privacy/egress constraints;
- measure the current triage baseline;
- define a fixed-scope pilot and acceptance criteria.

## Activities

1. Up to `[number]` stakeholder interviews.
2. One scope and data-flow workshop.
3. Read-only inspection of supplied repository metadata and synthetic or
   approved scanner samples.
4. Architecture, threat and operational analysis.
5. Pilot backlog, measurement plan and commercial recommendation.

## Deliverables

- signed scope manifest;
- threat model and data-flow diagram;
- scanner/integration and ownership inventory;
- egress, retention, secrets and approval policy;
- baseline metrics and pilot acceptance criteria;
- fixed-price pilot statement of work.

## Exclusions

No live production testing, exploitation, compliance certification, autonomous
publication or remediation guarantee is included.
