## Sovereign OSS Assurance

Government and regulated engineering teams rarely lack scanners. They lack a
controlled route from fragmented scanner output to an owned, reviewable and
auditable remediation decision.

Kleine Koe EvidenceFlow processes repository and scanner evidence inside
customer-controlled infrastructure. Deterministic tools remain the facts
source. A local model can deduplicate, summarize and propose remediation, but
every citation is checked against supplied evidence and every consequential
action requires a named reviewer.

### Pilot

- 10–20 explicitly authorized repositories;
- repository-policy, SARIF, Semgrep, Gitleaks and offline OSV ingestion;
- network-isolated scanner execution;
- local AI triage with fail-closed validation;
- private evidence packs, management summaries and audit receipts;
- no production probing and no autonomous external publication.

### Outcomes we measure

- human triage time;
- duplicates and stale findings;
- findings with an assigned owner and disposition;
- accepted remediation tests or pull requests;
- citation validity and rejected model output;
- external egress events, targeted at zero for local-only scopes.
