The operational gap

Your scanners find signals. Someone still has to turn them into decisions.

EvidenceFlow does not replace SAST, dependency or secret scanners. It creates a controlled path after they run.

1

Preserve the facts

Raw SARIF and repository observations remain separate from model-generated summaries.

2

Process locally

Restricted scopes use local model endpoints and network-isolated scanner processes.

3

Validate every claim

Structured output, exact source citations and bounded failure behavior stop plausible prose becoming evidence.

4

Keep people accountable

A named reviewer approves the result before an export, ticket or remediation handoff exists.

Evidence-first workflow

Deterministic where possible. AI where useful. Human where consequential.

01 · SCOPE

Authorized repositories, checks, expiry and egress policy.

02 · SCAN

Repository policy, supplied SARIF, Semgrep, Gitleaks and offline OSV.

03 · TRIAGE

Local AI correlates evidence and proposes context and remediation.

04 · REVIEW

A qualified reviewer validates severity, reachability and ownership.

05 · EXPORT

One idempotent, approved handoff with a tamper-evident audit trail.

Questions

Clear boundaries are part of the product.

Does this replace our security scanners?

No. Existing scanners remain the facts source. EvidenceFlow normalizes their output and controls the triage, review and evidence handoff.

Can source code stay inside our infrastructure?

Yes. A restricted pilot uses local checkouts, local model endpoints and network-isolated scanner processes. The deployment design still needs customer approval.

Does AI automatically open tickets or pull requests?

No. The pilot stops at a named approval boundary. External connectors are only added with explicit authorization and idempotent controls.

Can you guarantee that every vulnerability is found?

No credible provider can. We contract for defined scope, evidence handling, review quality and measurable workflow outcomes.
